# Combat powers of rare equipment

Existing attribute and flat attack bonuses remain unchanged. All epic, legendary and special equippable gear now also grants combat powers. Mounts, entry tokens and consumables retain their separate systems.

| Rarity | Both damage types | Armor per piece | Echo | Life steal | Active ability |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Epic | +5% | +1 | Every fourth attacking turn, +20% damage | None | None |
| Legendary | +10% | +2 | Every third attacking turn, +35% damage | 5% | Relic Strike: 160% of stronger attack, 8 stamina, 6-turn cooldown |
| Special | +15% | +3 | Every third attacking turn, +50% damage | 8% | Cataclysm: 200% of stronger attack, 8 stamina, 5-turn cooldown; burn for three turns at 10% of stronger attack per turn |

Damage percentage, echo, life steal and the active ability use only the highest equipped rarity. Armor stacks up to +12. Bag items grant nothing. Attributes, existing flat attack and set bonuses still stack normally. The percentage increases derived physical and magical attack before class skill multipliers.

Each damaging action counts as one hit toward echo, including item and offensive class skills. Guard, potion, recovery, non-damaging skills and ongoing damage do not count. Echo never recursively triggers. Life steal uses actual direct damage (including echo), capped at remaining enemy HP and player maximum HP; ongoing damage grants no healing. Burning replaces weaker burning rather than stacking duplicate burns.

Counters and cooldowns persist inside the saved battle. Invalid or premature ability attempts spend no turn or stamina. A cast on turn 1 is ready again on turn 6 (special) or 7 (legendary). Higher-rarity raid rewards remain subject to existing source restrictions; raids themselves are still preparation-only.

SK/EN descriptions appear in shared item inspection and equipped-slot dialogs; combat buttons and logs are bilingual. Drop rates, prices and equip requirements are unchanged.
