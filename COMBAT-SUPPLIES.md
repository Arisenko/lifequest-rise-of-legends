# Prepared combat supplies

The backpack has All, Weapons, Armor, Consumables and Other filters. Filtering preserves original inventory indices, so duplicate copies remain independent.

Prepare consumables before combat. The belt holds three individual copies in total, or five for the Alchemist, including repeated copies of the same item. Prepared copies move out of the backpack, retain carried weight and cannot be sold from the backpack. Unequipping requires a free backpack slot.

Only prepared supplies can be used during ordinary, dungeon and travel combat. Using one consumes one turn and exactly one prepared copy. Refilling or changing the belt during active combat is blocked by the action layer. Backpack consumables remain usable outside combat.

Prepared supplies are saved and validated. Existing saves retain their backpack contents; nothing is automatically equipped.
