# Mount rewards

The stable sells only the original mule, horse and stag.
The six new mounts are inventory/equipment items, not stable purchases.
All benefits apply only while equipped. Legendary mounts save 50% entry energy and have distinct attributes/passives; the Special drake saves 70%.
Boar: +8 Strength, +4 Vitality, +25kg capacity. Wolf: +4 Strength, +8 Discipline, +20 stamina. Ram: +8 Vitality, +4 Discipline, +30 maximum energy. Bear: +10 Vitality, +4 Strength, +4 armor. Gryphon: +8 Intelligence, +4 Discipline, 1.5× energy regeneration.
Drake: +10 Strength/Intelligence, +8 Discipline, +12 Vitality, +40kg capacity, +30 stamina, +40 maximum energy, 2× energy regeneration and immunity to the existing burn damage-over-time effect. Direct attacks and poison still hurt. Attribute bonuses affect combat through existing stat formulas.
Regeneration bonuses also apply while resting; energy entry costs round upward to an integer and never fall below one. Integer percentage arithmetic avoids a 70% discount charging 31 instead of 30 on a base cost of 100.
Dungeon legendary mounts: boar (level 5), wolf (20), ram (40), bear (55), gryphon (70).
Each belongs to a distinct dungeon boss: boar → Ravenmoor Lord; wolf → Ancient Shade; ram → Drowned King; bear → Emberlord; gryphon → Astral Oracle. Dungeon identity and boss checks prevent cross-dungeon mount drops.
The legendary tier shares a 0.3% roll with other eligible legendary rewards; an item is then chosen uniformly. This is not 0.3% per mount.
The drake is Special (red, highest rarity), level 90, reserved for Ashen Tyrant in the planned first raid. Its planned tier chance is 0.1%. There is no playable raid yet; it cannot currently drop.
Previewing a reward never grants an item or consumes entry tokens.
Minimum level limits equipping, not dropping. Existing inventory limits and overflow storage still apply.
Prices on reward mounts are resale valuation, not purchase offers. Direct stable purchase rejects reward mounts.
Drop selection checks both boss identity and dungeon/raid mode; adventure bosses, elites and training cannot award these mounts.

## Art

Shared prompt: stylized-concept, one full-body saddled mount, three-quarter standing pose, hand-painted Dark Cartoon Fantasy matching `items-clean-v2.png`, bold outlines, textured materials, burgundy/brass accents, no text/scenery/frame, 15% transparent margins.
