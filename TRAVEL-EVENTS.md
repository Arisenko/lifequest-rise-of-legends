# Events between encounters

Pending events open in a centered native modal dialog with a dimmed, blurred backdrop, scrollable mobile sizing and automatic focus. Background controls are inert. Escape/backdrop cannot dismiss an unresolved event; the combat gate also remains enforced in game state. Resolving shows a second modal view with actual resource changes and a Continue button. Outcome dismissal does not grant anything again; a compact receipt remains on the route. Old receipts do not reopen automatically. Browser checks cover Escape, resolution and returning to the route without touching player saves.

On the first victory over each non-final adventure opponent, or each non-final dungeon room, a 35% roll may create a pending event. Previously defeated adventure opponents do not farm extra events. Dungeon events are new for each key-paid run. There is no event after the final boss.

Six bilingual events: wire trap (Discipline), buried cache (Strength), quiet spring (Vitality), forgotten runes (Intelligence), lost traveler (Strength), wayside shrine (Discipline). Cards reuse painted combat icons and show risk, success chance and rewards before choosing. Events are mandatory: there is no bypass button, and the resolver also rejects bypass requests. Legacy safe-choice receipts remain readable. Peaceful choices restore at most 15% max HP and 8 energy. Other choices use a stat-based 50–90% chance, grant modest level-scaled gold or XP, and may cost 8% max HP on failure without reducing HP below 1. The cache also places a healing herb in the reward chest; no rarity or boss-only loot rules are bypassed.

Event kind and outcome seed are rolled once during the victorious state transition, outside the React updater. They persist with the save. Reloading or changing tabs never rolls another event. A pending event blocks all new expedition fights and dungeon entry until resolved. Resolution verifies the pending ID, refuses active combat and dead characters, clears pending state and stores a bounded 20-entry receipt history atomically, preventing duplicate rewards. Existing battle-reward receipts remain unchanged. Abandoning a dungeon discards its pending event without reward and keeps existing key/cooldown rules.

The shared mode type supports raids and has tests for that mode. Raids remain explicitly unplayable in the current project: no fabricated raid battles, seal charges or raid-event rewards are enabled. The actual raid encounter loop will use the same event factory/resolver once implemented.

Save validation checks pending events and receipts. Old saves with neither field continue working without migration. SK/EN UI, save round trips, deterministic resolution, rejected bypass requests, duplicate claims, combat locks, capped healing/energy and bounded history are covered by tests.
