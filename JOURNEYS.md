# Travel between adventures

See `ROUTE-MAPS.md` for the current acquired-map puzzle. Automatic journal hints were removed: the exact route appears only with an owned map, and blind guesses are checked only after the complete five-choice sequence.

Travel gates the first visit to each unlocked adventure after the starter region. Existing cleared/visited areas retain progress. Five correct choices grant 15 × starting level XP once and mark the destination visited. Level 100 receives no XP. Returning to the overview preserves unfinished puzzles. Active combat, open dungeons, unresolved mandatory events and dead characters prevent progress. Native buttons, live counters, focus styles and responsive layouts support SK/EN.

Travel hazards use normal combat and health. Surviving a hazard accepts its precise stage/row as an alternate route; fatal injuries, defeat and retreat do not. See ROUTE-MAPS.md. Each destination requires its own reusable map.
